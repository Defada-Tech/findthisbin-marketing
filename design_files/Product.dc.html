Anatomy of a lookup

One request in. A complete card record out.

Send a BIN to the engine running in your environment, get back every attribute your stack needs — each one typed, versioned, and carrying its own confidence score. No stitching together raw network files.

→
Identity: scheme, type, category, product / card level.
→
Issuer: name, country, and settlement currency.
→
Flags & DCC: prepaid, commercial, regulated-debit, DCC eligibility.
→
Provenance: per-field confidence and dataset version.

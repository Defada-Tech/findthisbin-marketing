Why we exist

BIN data was stuck in the 6-digit era. Payments moved on.

As card ranges migrated to 8 digits and EU regulation reshaped disclosure and DCC, the legacy BIN tables most providers still ship quietly fell out of date — mislabeling prepaid and commercial cards, guessing at issuer country, and ignoring residency entirely.

We started FindThisBIN to fix that from first principles: true 8-digit precision, multi-source corrections, per-field confidence, and a sub-10ms API hosted entirely in the EU — built by people who have shipped payments infrastructure and know what a checkout hot path demands.

Today that data powers routing, surcharging, DCC, checkout UX, and risk for modern payment platforms across the continent.
